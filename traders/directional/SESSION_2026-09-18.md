## 5. Why exits paid USDC.e instead of pUSD

**Buys** use CLOB **v2** FOK (`buy_token` → pUSD, spenders `0xE111…` / collateral adapters).

**Sells** (GTC/FAK) of these outcome tokens credit **USDC.e** (`0x2791…`). Evidence:

- Start USDC.e **$0**, now **$83.82**.
- Start pUSD **$134.94**, now **$1.07**.
- No other wrap/unwrap job was run.

On-chain, 5m up/down CTF positions are still **USDC.e-collateralized** Conditional Tokens. v2 matching uses `CtfCollateralAdapter` (`0xAdA100…`) so a **buy** wraps pUSD → USDC.e into CTF. A **sell** unwinds CTF → **USDC.e out**, not pUSD. The onramp (`CollateralOnramp` `0x93070a…`) is buy-side only; there is no auto-wrap on sell.

First hours also posted GTD/FAK on the **v1** `create_order` path (`invalid order version` until we switched). v1 quote asset is USDC.e by definition.

So even a “correct” v2 GTC on this tape **returns USDC.e**. Next FOK then fails: v2 buyer only sees pUSD.

**Ops fix:** after every sell (or on a timer), `wrap_usdce_to_pusd`. Before next FOK, require `pUSD ≥ $2` or skip. Do not keep firing FOK at $1.07.

## 6. Bugs that made P&amp;L worse than the formula

1. **Entry fill counted as exit.** For a taker buy, `is_buy_yes` is false, and `ours + token match` closes the slot. Several `filled_exit` stamps are the **same second** as `bet`. We then think we are flat, GTC may still rest, and we open another ticket (cap of 3 is on the *log*, not chain).
2. **GTC before credit** (fixed mid-session): sell with balance 0.
3. **Inferred tick 0.0001 vs book 0.01** (partially fixed): GTC rejected, dump FAK rejected.
4. **Dump spam** into resolved empty books; 429 on Polynode.
5. **No fee skip, no crypto denylist** despite both existing in research.
6. **$2 / 1¢ = 200 shares.** Ban `last &lt; 0.10` (or `n &gt; 20`).
7. **Always-on + 5m resolve.** Inventory held into YES=0 is a 100% loss. Need a hard flatten before window end, or do not trade windows that resolve inside H=30.
8. **RPC listen** lost condition_id / outcome / tick_size (`tok:` keys, ~19% fills dropped as `px∉(0,1)`). Polynode is required for this formula; 429 needs a single shared hub and slower reconnect.
